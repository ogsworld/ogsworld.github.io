Privacy policy

rclone INDOMINUS is used only by its owner, Oscar Gonzalez, to back up his own Google Drive.

  • It asks Google for read-only access to the owner's Drive.
  • Files are copied only to the owner's own computer.
  • No data is collected from anyone else, and nothing is shared, sold or sent to any other service.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
